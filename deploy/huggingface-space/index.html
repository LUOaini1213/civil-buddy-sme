Recorded example

Clause 4.8 · 40HQ · Partial: in the repository’s synthetic example, the recorded plan reports 6 × 40HQ for 24 pieces and 10,800 kg net.

Clause 4.9 · gross mass · Partial: a recorded heaviest container of 6,472.8 kg gross against this synthetic tender’s 20,000 kg limit.

Calculation limits: A-frame stillages are not modelled. Gross mass uses an approximate 40HQ tare and excludes dunnage, lashing and stillage weight. These figures do not establish a final shipping plan or tender compliance. A competent person must review and sign off the loading plan before booking; the signed verified gross mass (VGM) governs.

Those numbers are a snapshot from the published example; they are not calculated by this page. The source repository contains the reproducible offline command and test data.

Human review remains essential

  • Lashing, handling and crate structure require competent review.
  • Qualifications and price remain for people to fill in.
  • Generated drafts do not book containers or submit a tender.
