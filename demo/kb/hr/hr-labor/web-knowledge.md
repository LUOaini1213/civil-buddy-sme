## SG（2026-08-28 打开 MOM / TADM）

- MOM 页题 *About the Employment Act*（Last Updated: 14 July 2026）：新加坡主要劳动法；基本条款与工作条件，有例外。https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/employment-act ；SSO *Employment Act 1968*：https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/EmA1968
- MOM 页题 *Employment Act: who it covers*（Last Updated: 24 July 2025）：合同 of service 下的本地与外劳雇员一般覆盖；海员、家政、法定机构/公务员除外。持工作准证者另受 *Employment of Foreign Manpower Act*。Part 4（休息日、工时及其他服务条件）只覆盖页述薪资门槛内的 workman / 非管理人员，**不覆盖** managers or executives。门槛以当页为准，不把页述数字抄进检查表当岗位行情。https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/employment-act/who-is-covered
- MOM 页题 *What is a contract of service*（Last Updated: 29 December 2025）：employer-employee；可书面/口头/明示/默示。**contract of service ≠ contract for service**（独立承揽 / 自雇）。没有单一结论性测试；观察点：控制、生产资料归属、经济风险。本岗只列观察点，不宣布「其实就是雇员」。https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/contract-of-service/what-is-a-contract-of-service
- MOM 页题 *Key employment terms (KETs)*（Last Updated: 14 March 2024）：2016-04-01 后订约、受 Employment Act 覆盖、雇期 14 天及以上，须书面 KETs，开工后 14 日内发出。页列 1–18 项（雇主/雇员全名、职务与主要职责、开工日、期限、工时安排、薪期、基本薪、津贴与扣款、加班、假期与医疗、试用与通知、可选工作地点等）。PME 不适用加班则可省略加班两栏。数字用户未给则待填。https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/contract-of-service/key-employment-terms
- MOM 页题 *Paying salary*（Last Updated: 30 January 2026）：至少每月一次；薪期结束后 **7 日**内；加班费 **14 日**内。页述 **Singapore does not have a minimum wage**，薪酬由双方议定。欠薪争议走 TADM，不由本岗代判。https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/salary/paying-salary
- MOM 页题 *Paying the salary of foreign worker*（Last Updated: 14 March 2024）：须按向 MOM 申报的月薪支付，不迟于该月最后一日后 7 日；宿舍工人须入本人银行账户；须留月薪记录；外劳一般不缴 CPF。https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/paying-the-salary
- TADM 页题 *Mediation and advice for managing employment or payment-related disputes*：就业或薪酬争议调解与咨询；有申报时限。https://www.tal.sg/tadm
- CPF 雇主义务（仅公民/PR）：https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/employer-obligations
