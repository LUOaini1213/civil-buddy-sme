## SG · BCA 场地调查 / 工前普查

- Building Control Regulations 2003 r.31 Site investigation：拟做基础、隧道、场地平整（含开挖）或相关土方时，结构 QP 须调查场地以确定地基与地下水；须按 relevant standards and codes of practice，用足够钻孔/原位/室内试验，并由 SAC 认可的 Site Investigation 检验机构实施；SI 报告交 Commissioner，并附专业工程师核实准确性的证明。https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/BCA1989-S666-2003
- 同规 r.32 Pre-construction survey：拆除、打桩、基础、隧道或场地平整开工前，builder 须对邻近既有建筑做工前状况普查，把报告交给业主，并在现场留全套。
- Guidelines for ST Plan Applications（页更 2026-07-15）页列：GeoSS Guide on Ground Investigation and Geotechnical Characteristic Values to EC7；Guidelines on Pre-Construction Survey Prior to Carrying out Construction Works；Guideline on requirements for site investigation reports；AGS(SG) 电子格式。新增可见题名：Earth Retaining or Stabilising Structures – Requirements for Professional Engineers (Geo) and Accredited Checkers (Geo)（2026-06）；Geotechnical Building Works Requirements for Professional Engineers (Geo) and Accredited Checkers (Geo)（2026-06）；Guideline on ensuring integrity of sheet pile wall to prevent ground loss（2025-11-27）。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/safety-and-standards/applications-and-licenses/structural-plan-submission/guidelines-for-st-plan-applications/
- Structural Plan submission（页更 2026-05-07）：结构计划批准后、结构工事开工前须取得 Permit to Carry Out Structural Works。CORENET X 强制范围以 APPBCA-2026-12 为准（2026-10-01 起新项目 GFA≥5,000 m²），不得再写「全部新项目」。本岗放样稿不是该许可。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/safety-and-standards/applications-and-licenses/structural-plan-submission/
- 工前普查落地页（页更 2026-01-26）：拆除/打桩/基础/隧道/场地平整前须做。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/safety-and-standards/applications-and-licenses/permit-for-structural-works/pre-construction-survey-and-communication-plan-for-landed-development/
- 现场须留 QP 核证的 SI 与土工试验报告；试验室须 SAC-SINGLAS。BCA construction site records（页更 2026-01-29）：https://www1.bca.gov.sg/safety-and-standards/applications-and-licenses/permit-for-structural-works/construction-site-records/
