## SG

- Building Control Act 1989（目录：s.7 Supervision of building works；s.8–11 发展商 / QP / site supervisor / builder 职责）：https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/BCA1989
- Building Control Regulations 2003：https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/BCA1989-S666-2003
- BCA 页题 *Site Supervisor requirements*（页更 2026-03-12）：permit 时由 Qualified Person（QP）委任 site supervisor。职称：Resident Engineer（RE）、Resident Technical Officer（RTO）。估价不超过 S$7.5m 可为 QP 或 RE 或 RTO；超过则按估价全职配置 RE/RTO。本岗只列职称，不代签。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/safety-and-standards/applications-and-licenses/permit-for-structural-works/requirements-on-site-supervisor-and-framework-for-risk-based-inspection/
- IES 页题 *IES/ACES C&S RE/RTO Registry*：结构现场监督须认可为 C&S RE 或 RTO；持 PEB 执业证的 PE 以 PE 号在 CORENET 登记即可。https://www.ies.org.sg/registries/ies-aces-c-s-re-rto-registry/
- BCA 页题 *Construction site records*（页更 2026-01-29）：QP 须在工地保管 site and test records（含 site record book）。清单 https://go.gov.sg/site-form 。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/safety-and-standards/applications-and-licenses/permit-for-structural-works/construction-site-records/
- BCA 页题 *Record Structural Plan (C-Forms)*（页更 2026-01-26）：immaterial change 竣工后交 record plans；material change 须先交 amendment plans。C 表官方全名：Form C1 Certificate of Supervision of Piling Works and Notice of Completion；Form C2 Certificate of Supervision of Structural Works；Form C3 Submission Certificate of Record Structural Plans/Calculations。桩基 as-built 完成桩基后 28 日内。同页曾写 CORENET X 对全部新项目 2026-10-01 强制；APPBCA-2026-12（2026-07-23）已收窄为新项目 GFA≥5,000 m²。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/safety-and-standards/applications-and-licenses/permit-for-structural-works/record-structural-plan-c-forms/
- TOP/CSC（页更 2026-06-30）：由发展商委任的 QP 申请；清单含 Certificate of Supervision、Builder's Certificate、As-built Plans、C1–C3 等。本岗只列目录，不宣布可领 TOP/CSC。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/safety-and-standards/applications-and-licenses/application-for-top-csc/
- CORENET X 竣工网关交 as-built：https://info.corenet.gov.sg/
