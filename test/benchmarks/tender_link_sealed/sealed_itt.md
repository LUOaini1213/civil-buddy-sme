SECTION 4 PARTICULAR SPECIFICATION - PACKING AND DELIVERY

4.8 Containers: Unitised panels shall be shipped from the factory in 40ft high cube containers. The gross mass of each loaded container shall not exceed 26,000 kg.

4.8.1 Only 40' HC containers will be accepted at the site unloading bay. Cargo weight per container, excluding dunnage, shall not exceed 22 t.

4.9 Delivery containers

(a) Aluminium cladding panels shall be delivered in 20-foot general purpose containers; the payload of each container shall not exceed 18 tonnes.

(b) Stillages and crates shall be lashed to the container's lashing points using rated webbing straps, and all voids shall be filled with dunnage or inflatable dunnage bags so that no load can shift in transit.

(c) Packing and securing of every container shall comply with the IMO/ILO/UNECE Code of Practice for Packing of Cargo Transport Units (CTU Code, 2014).

4.10 Unitised panels shall be carried upright on steel A-frame stillages with glass faces protected by foam pads and corner protectors. Glazed panels shall not be stacked or laid flat. The loaded mass of each A-frame stillage shall not exceed 2,500 kg.

4.11 The weight of cargo, including dunnage and bracing, shall not exceed 24,000 kg in any 40' high-cube container.

4.12 Bracing 加固：A-frame 与柜壁之间用不小于 75 × 75 mm 的 timber bracing 顶紧，任何荷载不得压在玻璃上。

4.13 All timber used in crates, pallets, bracing and dunnage shall be treated and marked in accordance with ISPM 15. No crate shall exceed 1,200 kg gross.

4.14 Mullions and transoms longer than 6 m shall be packed in returnable steel frames; timber crates are not accepted for these items.

4.15 Insurance: The Subcontractor shall maintain Contractor's All Risks and Work Injury Compensation insurance for the duration of the Subcontract.

4.16 Marine cargo insurance shall cover all materials from the factory to site, including while stored in containers at port, for 110% of the invoice value.

4.17 Warranty: 10 years for water tightness and 20 years for the PVDF coating of aluminium; damage in transport does not shorten the warranty period.

SECTION 5 LOGISTICS

The Subcontractor shall ship all façade materials in 40HQ containers, and the loaded weight of each container, container tare included, shall not exceed 30 tonnes.

5.1 Containers may be either 20GP or 40HQ, at the Subcontractor's option, provided the loading arrangement is approved by the Main Contractor.

5.2 All materials shall be delivered in 40-foot containers.

5.3 Container 要求：统一使用 40HQ（高柜），每柜 cargo net weight 不超过 20 吨，不含 A-frame 及 dunnage。

5.4 Open top and flat rack containers shall not be used.

5.5 Container 柜型：20' GP 或 40' GP；因现场入口限高，不接受 high cube 柜。

5.6 20GP containers shall not be used for unitised panels; panels shall travel in 40ft high cube containers only.

PART C EMPLOYER'S REQUIREMENTS

Clause 12 Transport of Glazed Units. Oversized glazed canopy units shall be transported on 40ft flat rack containers with weather-proof covers.

Clause 13 Long Members. Steel canopy beams exceeding 11.5 m shall be shipped in 40ft open top containers and loaded by crane.

Clause 14 Hoisting at Site. The tower crane at the loading bay is rated 3.2 t at 40 m radius; no single lift, including spreader and slings, shall exceed 3,000 kg.

Clause 15 Site Access. Delivery vehicles entering from Synthetic Avenue shall not exceed 32 tonnes gross vehicle weight; prime movers with 40ft trailers must obtain the Main Contractor's slot booking 48 hours ahead.

Clause 16 Delivery Sequence. Panels shall arrive in installation sequence, floor by floor and elevation by elevation, as per the approved installation programme. Each container shall carry panels for one floor only.

Clause 17 Logistics Plan. The Subcontractor shall submit a packing and logistics plan stating container type and number, loading arrangement, weight per container and centre of gravity, securing method and delivery schedule, not later than 4 weeks before the first shipment.
